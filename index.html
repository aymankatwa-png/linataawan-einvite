To the European Union Delegation to Libya,

Moomken Organization cordially invites the European Union Delegation to Libya to attend a community dialogue titled “Women's Leadership and Ending Violence Against Women”, held as part of the Linataawan III project during the 16 Days of Activism.

We would be honored by your presence on Monday, 30 November 2026, from 10:00 to 13:00, at the Tripoli Resource Center, Hay Al-Andalus.

Moomken Organizationfor Awareness & Media
on behalf of the Linataawan III project
What participants will do

From barriers
to safer action.

The dialogue will bring together 30 female participants from civil society organizations to examine barriers to women's leadership and discuss how CSOs can respond safely when women seek support related to violence. Through a facilitated exercise, participants will identify practical responses, consider how CSOs can engage men and boys in prevention, and agree on recommendations and possible joint actions.

The group exercise

50 minutes · small groups · fictional scenarios

Each group leaves with one key barrier, one proposed safe response and one practical joint action for the 16 Days of Activism and beyond.
