# الميدان (Al-Maydan): VR journey through Libyan civil society

Open `index.html` from an **https** address in the Meta Quest Browser and press "ادخل الواقع الافتراضي".
Works with hand tracking (touch or pinch) and with controllers (point and trigger). On a PC or phone, use "جرّب على الشاشة".

- Story text, years and tasks: the `ERAS` list at the top of `main.js`.
- Colors of each time period: the `ENV` list in `main.js`.
- Art: `img/` (approved RC stickers and logos). Sound: `aud/`.
